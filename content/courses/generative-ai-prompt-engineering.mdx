## Overview

This comprehensive generative AI course is designed for students with no prior experience in AI/ML. You'll learn to work with Large Language Models (LLMs), master prompt engineering techniques, build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, and create autonomous AI agents.

By the end of this course, you'll be able to design and deploy production-ready generative AI applications using modern frameworks and best practices.

## Who Should Take This Course

- **Beginners** interested in generative AI and LLMs
- **Developers** wanting to integrate AI capabilities into applications
- **Product managers** seeking hands-on AI experience
- **Students** preparing for careers in AI/ML engineering
- **Anyone** looking to understand how modern AI systems work

No prior AI/ML experience required. Basic programming knowledge is helpful but not mandatory.

## Target audience

Students with no prior experience in AI/ML.

Hands-on sessions facilitated by the instructor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need prior AI/ML experience?

No! This course is designed for complete beginners. We start with foundational concepts and build up to advanced topics step by step.

### What programming languages will we use?

Primarily Python. If you're new to Python, we recommend taking our optional Python Fundamentals course first.

### How long does the course take?

This course is part of our 2-month comprehensive AI training program. Classes are held multiple times per week with hands-on projects and assignments.

### Will I get hands-on experience?

Yes! Every module includes practical exercises, and the course culminates in a capstone project where you'll build a complete generative AI application.

### What's the difference between this and the MLOps course?

This course focuses on generative AI, LLMs, and prompt engineering. The MLOps course covers the full ML lifecycle, model deployment, and production infrastructure. We recommend taking both for comprehensive AI/ML skills.

### Can I work while taking this course?

Yes, many of our students work full-time. We offer flexible scheduling and recorded sessions for those who need to balance work and learning.

### What tools and platforms will I use?

You'll work with OpenAI APIs, LangChain, vector databases like Pinecone and Chroma, and modern Python development tools. All necessary accounts and access will be provided.

### Will I receive a certificate?

Yes, you'll receive a certificate of completion from Monk Technologies upon successfully finishing the course and capstone project.
