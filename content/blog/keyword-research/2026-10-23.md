## Notes for Writing
- Cite specific sources for claims (Gartner reports, vendor announcements, job posting data)
- Use clear, concrete examples (email agent, research agent, customer service agent)
- No fabricated statistics or testimonials
- Avoid overhyping ("agents will replace all jobs") or underhyping ("just fancy chatbots")
- Explain technical concepts (tool use, planning, memory) without requiring ML background
- Balance accessibility with accuracy
- Natural course integration - educational first, promotional second
- Include visual/conceptual descriptions (agent loop: perceive → plan → act → learn)
