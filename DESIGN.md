# Monk Technologies — visual design brief

Training-first AI academy. Lead users of AI, not a diversity campaign.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep indigo | `#1a1a5e` | Dark surfaces, footer, hero panel |
| Electric blue | `#0066ff` | Emphasis, secondary CTA, links |
| Warm gold | `#ffb800` | Primary CTA |
| Accent green | `#00c853` | Success / live indicators |
| Canvas | `#f4f4fb` | Page background |

## Type

- Headlines: Archivo (600–800), tight tracking
- Body: Inter
- Code: JetBrains Mono

## Layout

Mobile-first, max width 72rem, generous section spacing, gold/blue CTAs above the fold. Neural-network motif as background geometry—not decoration that replaces people.

## Imagery

Prefer real community photos with permission. Until then: no stock faces. Use initials, skin-tone discs, and instructor role cards. Show builders and critics, not consumers of AI.

## Voice

Empowering, joyful, intelligent. No struggle porn, no fake AAVE, no salary promises. The community is the lead user of this product year-round.
